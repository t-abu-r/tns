Frequently Asked Questions

Applicants must have passed Cambridge O-Level / IGCSE or Matriculation with a minimum of 5 passes including English and Mathematics. Students applying for Pre-Medical or Pre-Engineering streams require B grades or above in relevant science subjects.
Scholarships are based on O-Level grade point totals out of 48 (Matrix: A* = 6, A = 5, B = 4, C = 3, D = 2, E = 1). Tiers include 100% Scholarship for 48 Points; 95% off for 45 Points; 80% off for 40 Points; 60% off for 30 Points; 50% off for 25 Points; and 40% off for 20 Points. A special 50% monthly tuition discount is also granted for teacher's children.
Our dedicated Career & University Counseling Office provides end-to-end guidance for UCAS (UK), Common App (US), Canadian universities, SAT/ACT prep workshops, and interview coaching.
Subject changes are permitted within the first 3 weeks of the academic term, subject to subject counselor approval and class availability.
